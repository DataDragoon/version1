WHAT CHANGED vs v14
-------------------
    rx.vhd              DSP_FIFO_WORDS 51 -> 255. The FIFO's RAM is 256
                        deep (pointer space is a power of two); 255 usable
                        because dsp_sweep_words, the stepper's num_steps
                        and the gate threshold are 8-bit. Assert on the
                        width. 2 x 255 = 510 DWORDs still fits one GPIF
                        transfer (1024 at High Speed), so the FX3 read is
                        unchanged: one burst, constant tail after it.
    sweep_stepper.vhd   TABLE_AW 12 -> 14: 16 KB, 255 steps x 64 bytes.
                        The op-0 table-write address moves from bits 27:16
                        to bits 27:8 (14 bits needed); words_written and
                        status bits 29:16 grow to 14 bits.
    bladerf-hosted.vhd  TABLE_AW => 14.
    pkt_retune2.c       STP_MAX_STEPS 64 -> 255; table words are
                        (addr << 8) | data; LOAD reply masks 14 bits.
    Host (version50 branch fifo-256): DSP_SWEEP_WORDS 255; the dsp core
                        accepts 2..255 steps with the stepper.

Resources: 9,236 ALMs (8 %), 349 M10K (29 %) -- the FIFO moved from MLAB

WHY
---
On v13 the FIFO gate opens at 51 words, so the sweep must be exactly 51
steps and the step size is tied to the span (60 MHz over 2-5 GHz). This
image lets the step count be a runtime choice from the Pi or the GUI:

    step      steps   unambiguous   sweep     rate
    20 MHz    151     7.5 m         27.8 ms   ~36 Hz
    40 MHz     76     3.75 m        14.0 ms   ~71 Hz
    60 MHz     51     2.5 m          9.4 ms   ~100 Hz  (today's setting)
    120 MHz    26     1.25 m         4.8 ms   ~190 Hz
    200 MHz    16     0.75 m         2.9 ms   ~280 Hz

Resolution is 5 cm throughout (same span). A 256-deep FIFO also holds
five 51-step sweeps, which a later image could use to let the stepper
free-run and remove the 0.64 ms EXEC + USB gap per sweep.

HOW TO USE
----------
    Pi, on the version50 fifo-256 checkout, services stopped:
      bladeRF-cli -l fpga/images/hostedxA9_niosIIf_sweep_dsp_v15_fifo256_signaltap.rbf
      python3 pi/radar/check_bit6.py
    start.py (dsp is the default mode), then set any step in the GUI or
    over the socket. Console:
      [sfcw] FPGA stepper table loaded: N steps
      [bladerf] DSP burst ... after EXEC
    On this image a sweep longer than 255 steps is refused by the engine;
    one longer than the fast-lock table (151 at 20 MHz) is refused by the
    grid snap before that.
