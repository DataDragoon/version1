WHAT CHANGED SINCE v5
---------------------
cw_mixer.vhd only. scale() was round, shift AND clamp in one function,
evaluated entirely between the DSP block's output register and out_*:

    out_i <= scale(acc_i);          -- +HALF, shift, 2 saturation compares, mux

Quartus infers a multiply-add per channel and absorbs both the products and the
adder into the DSP block, so that whole chain hung off Add1~mac_pl with nothing
to break it. Split into two stages:

    s_i_r <= round_shift(acc_i);    -- stage 2: +HALF and shift, registered at
                                    --   SHIFT_W = SUM_W-TONE_FRAC = 19 bits
    out_i <= clamp(s_i_r);          -- stage 3: two 19-bit compares and a mux

Registering at 19 bits rather than 33 also shrinks the stage-3 comparators. The
value provably fits -- 16x16 products, sum of two is 33 bits, >>14 leaves
exactly 19 -- so the narrower register is lossless and clamp() behaves
identically. Rounding and saturation are bit-for-bit unchanged.

Note what did NOT work and was not tried twice: adding a register between the
sum and scale() would have bought nothing, because the sum is already
registered inside the DSP block. The combinational length was scale() itself.

Costs one more clock: mixer latency is now 3 (multiply, round/shift, clamp).
out_valid is delayed to match. Both channels use identical instances, so the
accumulators stay in step and the ratio picks up no skew.

THROUGHPUT -- WHY THIS IMAGE WILL NOT CHANGE YOUR SWEEP RATE
------------------------------------------------------------
The measured 37 Hz on v5 is not caused by anything this image changes. As of
this build the Pi is still capturing RAW SAMPLES and reducing them itself:

    pi/radar/bladerf_driver.py   no dsp_path_enabled, no PACKET_META,
                                 no unpack_dsp_results, no SC16_Q11_PACKED
                                 -- zero occurrences of any of them
    pi/radar/sfcw_engine.py:972  start_rx_dual(self._rx_capture, num_samples=n)
    pi/radar/sfcw_engine.py:402  nios_dwell = 4096 samples per step

So the DSP result path has never actually run. dsp_path_en defaults to 0 and
nothing sets it. What has been measured on v5 is the ordinary raw-capture path,
which is also why the engine reports

    NIOS sweep: RX delivery lagged 86 ms at harvest -- possible ring overflow

86 ms is three sweeps of backlog, and the engine responds by discarding the
autonomous sweep and falling back to one standard sweep.

The acquisition ceiling is fixed and the DSP path cannot beat it, only reach it:

    51 steps x 4096 samples = 208,896 samples
    208,896 / 10 MS/s       = 20.89 ms  ->  47.9 Hz

37 Hz is 27.0 ms, so ~6.1 ms/sweep (~120 us/step) is overhead the DSP path
exists to delete: 51 x 4096 x 4 = 835 KB per sweep over USB becomes 408 bytes,
and the Pi stops computing 51 means and 51 divisions.

All three host-side changes from v4's list are still required, and none are in
place yet. Flashing v6 alone will measure 37 Hz again.

SEPARATE ISSUE SEEN IN THE SAME RUN
-----------------------------------
    RX ADC running hot -- RX1=2048/2047 (100% FS)

The front end is compressing. cw_mixer saturates rather than wraps by design,
but these samples are already clipped BEFORE they reach the FPGA, so the
accumulated result carries a bias no amount of averaging removes. Worth turning
RX1 gain down before trusting measurements from either image.
