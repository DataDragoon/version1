WHAT CHANGED vs v13
-------------------
    sweep_stepper.vhd   exports num_steps (the length the Nios loaded).
    rx.vhd              new input dsp_sweep_words (0 = DSP_FIFO_WORDS);
                        synchronised into the read clock; the gate opens at
                        2 x that many read-words; and while the gate is open
                        the FX3 is shown FULL (sample_fifo_rfull <=
                        dsp_fifo_ready) instead of the FIFO's own flag, so
                        fx3_gpif's (full & usedw) >= gpif_buf_size rule
                        fires for a partial-depth sweep.
    bladerf-hosted.vhd  wires the stepper's num_steps to rx, zeroed unless
                        control bit 29 (stepper) is set.
    Host (branch fewer-steps): the dsp core accepts 2..51 steps with the
                        stepper (was: exactly 51).

Simulation: sim_dsp_words (5 steps into an 8-deep FIFO, length 5 -> one
burst of the 5 results) PASS; on / off / resync / sel / stepper unchanged
and PASS.

WHY
---
Halving the step count halves the sweep at the same span (same 15 cm
resolution): 26 steps at a 40 MHz step over 2.0-3.0 GHz -> 26 x 1856
samples / 10.24 MS/s = 4.7 ms, ~190 Hz with the host pipelined, against
9.2 ms / 100 Hz at 51 steps. The price is the unambiguous range, c/(2 x
step): 7.5 m -> 3.75 m. It is a GUI setting (step size), nothing to build.

HOW TO USE
----------
    Pi, on the fewer-steps checkout, services stopped:
      bladeRF-cli -l .../fpga/images/hostedxA9_niosIIf_sweep_dsp_v14_steps_signaltap.rbf
      python3 radar/check_bit6.py
    start.py, set_sweep_mode.py dsp, then in the GUI set the step to 40 MHz
    (2.0-3.0 GHz -> 26 steps) and start the sweep. Console:
      [sfcw] FPGA stepper table loaded: 26 steps
      [bladerf] DSP burst 5 ms after EXEC
    At 20 MHz / 51 steps it is v13's 100 Hz configuration.
