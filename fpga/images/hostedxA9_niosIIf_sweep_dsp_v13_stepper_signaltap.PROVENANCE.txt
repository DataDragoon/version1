WHY THIS IMAGE EXISTS
---------------------
On v12 the step is bounded by the Nios: it preloads the next step's two
fast-lock profiles (36 SPI frames through software, ~270 us) inside the
dwell and jitters the step boundary by tens of microseconds, so the dwell
could not go below ~3456 samples (17.2 ms per sweep) whatever the DSP
chain needed. This image moves the retune sequence into the FPGA:

    sweep_stepper.vhd (80 MHz system clock)
      - 4 KB profile table, written by the Nios through the expansion PIO
        (control-register bit 29 hands the PIO to the stepper; the header
        pins are parked meanwhile; no Qsys change)
      - its own AD9361 SPI engine, CPOL 1 / CPHA 0 exactly as the Altera
        core the Nios uses (SCLK idle high, MOSI on the rising edge, sampled
        on the falling edge, MSB first, CS low for the whole frame), 20 MHz;
        muxed onto the adi_spi_* pins while a sweep runs (spi_owner)
      - per step: recall RX + TX (2 frames, ~2.8 us) at an exact tick, one
        restart toggle into the existing dsp_restart path, then preload of
        the next step (36 frames, ~90 us) during the dwell
      - frames are byte-for-byte what devices.c adi_fastlock_load() and
        adi_fastlock_recall() emit (sweep_stepper_tb checks all 114 of a
        3-step sweep)
    bladerf-hosted.vhd: stepper instance, SPI pin mux, PIO in/out mux,
        restart XOR, bit 29 readback
    pkt_retune2.c: SWLD ("copy the primed table into the FPGA"), SWEF ("run
        it, arg = dwell in 80 MHz ticks"; sets port/SPDT first), SWST also
        stops the stepper, SWQG carries its status.

The step is then FLUSH_N + ACCUM_N + a few samples: with the host branch's
512 + 1600 and a 128-sample guard, dwell 2240 -> 51 x 2240 / 10.24 MS/s =
11.2 ms per sweep (~89 Hz acquisition), Nios idle throughout.

WHAT IS NOT PROVEN
------------------
    - that the AD9361 accepts frames from this engine as it does from the
      Nios's. Same parameters, copied from the generated core, but never
      seen on a scope. If the first sweep produces no burst or wrong
      values, this is the first suspect; SCLK_DIV 4 -> 8 (10 MHz) is the
      first thing to try.
    - the table load through the PIO on hardware (the Nios's PIO writes
      are ~1 us each; 51 x 34 words = ~2 ms per prime).

HOW TO USE
----------
    Pi, on the fpga-stepper checkout, services stopped:
      bladeRF-cli -l .../fpga/images/hostedxA9_niosIIf_sweep_dsp_v13_stepper_signaltap.rbf
      python3 radar/check_bit6.py
    then start.py, set_sweep_mode.py dsp; expect on the console
      [sfcw] FPGA stepper table loaded: 51 steps
      [sfcw] DSP chain: FLUSH 512 + ACCUM 1600 = 2112 samples/step; dwell 2240 leaves 128 of guard
      [bladerf] DSP burst 11 ms after EXEC
    To fall back to the v12 behaviour on the same image: DSP_STEPPER = False
    in sfcw_engine.py (bit 29 stays clear; the Nios-timed sweep runs).
