WHY THIS IMAGE EXISTS -- what v10 showed on hardware
----------------------------------------------------
v10 was the first image whose DSP path was actually exercised on the board
(2026-09-11; every earlier run had the board's FLASH image loaded, and the
host had two faults of its own -- see version1 commits 0b6ff7b, 65f4e85).
Result: the path WORKS -- bursts of 51 Q14 ratios arrived 21 ms after EXEC,
the length of the sweep -- but only on 2 sweeps in 7. The other 5: no burst
within 1 s.

Cause, from the FPGA parameters: FLUSH_N + ACCUM_N = 1088 + 2900 = 3988
samples per step against a 4096-sample dwell -- 108 samples, ~11 us at
10 MS/s, for the Nios's profile_activate() (SPI writes to the AD9361) to
land the restart. It jitters by more than that. An early restart makes
seq_adder discard the part-accumulated step; the step produces no word; the
FIFO ends the sweep short of 51 and the gate (rx.vhd dsp_gate) never opens.
Worse, the leftover stayed in the FIFO, the NEXT sweep topped it up, and
that burst was the tail of one sweep and the head of the next: misaligned
data with nothing on the host able to tell.

WHAT CHANGED vs v10
-------------------
rx.vhd
  1. DSP_ACCUM_N 2900 -> 2400 (24 whole 100-sample tone cycles).
     Per-step need 3488 samples; 608 samples (~61 us) of guard at dwell
     4096. Cost 0.8 dB of per-point SNR. Sweep stays 21 ms / ~48 Hz.
  2. DSP FIFO, sweep counter (dsp_meta_writer) and gate (dsp_gate) are
     held CLEAR while dsp_path_en is low:
         dsp_fifo_aclr <= sample_fifo_raclr or not dsp_path_en;
     plus a second synchroniser (U_sync_dsp_path_en_rx) for the counter
     in the rx_clock domain. The host realigns the FIFO with the next
     sweep by dropping bit 6 and raising it -- bladerf_driver.dsp_resync()
     -- two GPIO writes, the RX stream untouched. One lost step costs one
     sweep, never the following ones. Nothing is collected while the path
     is deselected (priming retunes, for instance).
  3. dsp_in_reg: the mixers' input samples registered once. The rx mux
     (ADC / loopback / generator) is combinational and fed the DSP-block
     multiply directly; that was the first v11 fit's worst path (-0.233).
cw_nco.vhd
  4. cos_out/sin_out registered. phase -> 100-way ROM mux -> DSP multiply
     was the second fit's worst path (-0.543). The tone now trails the
     sample by one entry: a constant -2*pi/100 on the demodulated result,
     identical on both channels, cancels exactly in h.

Simulation (ModelSim 2020.1, rx_dsp_onoff_tb, real rx.vhd + real fx3_gpif):
    sim_dsp_on.do      5 steps, path on   : burst of 2048 DWORDs, 5 ratios
                       within 3 LSB of the reference, constant tail   PASS
    sim_dsp_off.do     path off           : raw samples as injected     PASS
    sim_dsp_resync.do  3 steps in (gate held, no burst), bit 6 1->0->1,
                       5 fresh steps: ONE burst, the 5 fresh values,
                       nothing of the 3 stale ones                     PASS

HOW TO USE
----------
    Pi, services stopped:
      bladeRF-cli -l /home/sfr/vikram_fpga_dump/version1/fpga/images/hostedxA9_niosIIf_sweep_dsp_v11_signaltap.rbf
      python3 radar/check_bit6.py        -> HOST, bit 6 reads back
    then start.py and
      python3 radar/test_dsp_path.py --seconds 20
    Expect every sweep 'dsp', "DSP burst 21 ms after EXEC" per sweep, and
    RESULT: PASS. A sweep that still misses now prints "no burst within
    ... ms" once and the next sweep starts from a cleared FIFO.

    The image is lost on power cycle (flash still holds the old 0.16.0
    image); check_bit6.py shows FLASH + bit 6 = 0 when that has happened.
